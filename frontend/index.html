Lane League

Garage

Select a car.

One quiet minute between recruiting and a problem set. Pick a car, empty your head, and leave the score on the board if you want company.

Rules

One car. One minute.

  • Your car. Only the car you picked is on the road. Your speed, in mph, stays on screen.
  • Time left. The clock in the middle counts down from one minute. Cross the finish before it hits zero.
  • Coins. Gold coins sit off the racing line. Steer into one for 100 points. There are 14.
  • Hurdles. Red and white barriers sit on the racing line. Hit one and the car slows down. Each hurdle takes 25 points off at the end. There are 10.
  • Ranked drive. A steward, a quiet passenger, and a clerk run that minute. Your name goes on the board with the score the server counted.
  • End. Stops the drive now and shows the score you have. There is no finish bonus.
  • Practice. Same road and the same one-minute clock, with no model call. The radio explains a coin and a hurdle. The score is not saved.

Hold ↑ to go. ← → to steer. ↓ to brake.

Safety

Instructions stop at the door.

A ranked minute calls the crew. Text that tries to give them an order never reaches the model, and it cannot move points.
